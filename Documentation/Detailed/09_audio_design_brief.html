09. Audio Design Brief

Dead City Escape — Production Document

Audio Direction

Sound should create fear, urgency, and co-op awareness. The player should understand danger through audio before seeing it.

Required Sounds

CategorySounds
WeaponsPistol, shotgun, rifle, reload, empty click, grenade.
ZombiesIdle growl, scream, attack, death, footsteps.
EnvironmentRain, wind, fire, sirens, electricity, metal creaks.
UIButton click, objective update, warning, lobby ready.
MusicExploration loop, combat loop, horde intensity, escape cue.

Implementation Notes

  • Use 3D spatial audio for zombies.
  • Use reverb zones in hospital and parking garage.
  • Layer music intensity based on zombie count.
  • Final horde should have sirens and heavy percussion.
