Studio Roles

  • Producer: schedule, budget, team communication.
  • Game designer: mechanics, balance, documentation.
  • Developer: Unity systems and multiplayer.
  • 3D artist: characters, environments, props.
  • Level designer: graybox, pacing, objectives.
  • Animator: player and zombie animations.
  • Sound designer: ambience, weapons, music.
  • QA: bugs, multiplayer testing, performance testing.
