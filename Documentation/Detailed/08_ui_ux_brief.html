HUD Elements

ElementPurpose
Health barShow player condition.
Teammate statusShow HP, downed, revive state.
Ammo counterCurrent mag and reserve ammo.
Objective textCurrent mission step.
Interaction promptDoors, pickups, revive, generator.
Damage indicatorShow attack direction.

Style

Dark, gritty, readable. Use red for danger, white for main text, yellow for objectives, green for successful interactions.
