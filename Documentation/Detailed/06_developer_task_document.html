Development Milestones

Programming Task List

  • Player movement controller.
  • Camera and aiming system.
  • Weapon fire, reload, ammo, recoil.
  • Health, damage, downed, revive.
  • Zombie AI state machine.
  • Spawner and horde director.
  • Objective manager.
  • Network lobby and room system.
  • Networked player and zombie sync.
  • HUD, inventory, interaction prompts.
  • Audio and VFX triggers.
  • Save/checkpoint system.

First Playable Demo Scope

  • One street section.
  • Two online players.
  • One objective.
  • Two weapons.
  • Two zombie types.
  • One final mini-horde.
