Session Length25–40 minutes per level

Core Fantasy

Two survivors are trapped inside a dead city after a zombie outbreak. They must loot, fight, solve objectives, restore power, and escape before the final horde overwhelms them.

Core Pillars

  • Co-op teamwork: revive, shared objectives, dual switches, resource sharing.
  • Survival tension: limited ammo, darkness, unpredictable zombie waves.
  • Cinematic escape: each level builds toward a large final survival event.
  • Readable combat: zombies must be visually clear and mechanically distinct.

Core Gameplay Loop

Explore area → collect resources → fight zombies → complete objective → unlock next area → survive escalation → escape

Main Objectives

  1. Escape starting alley.
  2. Find keycard in main street.
  3. Loot gun shop.
  4. Find fuse in hospital.
  5. Find fuel in underground parking.
  6. Restart generator.
  7. Survive final horde.
  8. Escape through city gate.
