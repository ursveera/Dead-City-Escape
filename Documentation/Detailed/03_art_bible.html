03. Art Bible

Dead City Escape — Production Document

Visual Direction

Realistic dark urban zombie outbreak. The world should feel wet, abandoned, dangerous, and cinematic.

Color Palette

  • Blue-gray night environment.
  • Orange fire highlights.
  • Red emergency lights.
  • Green toxic glow for exploder zombies.
  • Cold fluorescent hospital lighting.

Environment Mood

AreaMood
Start AlleyNarrow, dirty, tense.
Main StreetDestroyed city, emergency chaos.
Gun ShopLoot reward, broken glass, alarm danger.
HospitalHorror, darkness, gore, flickering lights.
Parking GarageConcrete, fog, headlights, ambushes.
Generator RoomIndustrial, red warning lights, sparks.
Final GateMilitary checkpoint, floodlights, horde arena.

PBR Material Rules

  • Use albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, and AO maps.
  • Wet surfaces should use higher reflection and darker albedo.
  • Blood decals should be layered and reusable.
  • Assets must be modular and reusable.
